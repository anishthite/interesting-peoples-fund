The Side Project Fund

It is not a fund. No checks, no equity, no term sheets. The only thing invested is a few hours of my week, and it goes to whoever reaches out with something they're building.

Stuck on a side project or a startup? Email me. That is the whole pitch.

§ 1

The Idea

I keep three or four hours a week open for people working on side projects and early startups. No application, no cohort, no demo day. Just a standing allocation of attention for anyone building something they care about.

A few focused hours from someone with no agenda turns out to be a surprisingly rare thing to ask for.

§ 2

How It Works

Reach out Cold emails welcome. Tell me what you're building and where you're stuck.
We talk A call, a coffee, a long email thread — whatever the problem wants.
I help Feedback, intros, a second pair of eyes on the code, a second opinion. Whatever a few hours can buy.
No strings No equity, no favors owed, no follow-up. Pay it forward if you ever feel like it.
